Treeview of a taxonomic tree

A Unipept taxonomic tree, drawn as a collapsible node–link diagram. Click a node to expand or collapse its subtree, and hover one for its hit count. colorProviderLevels: 3 assigns a distinct colour explicitly down to the third level instead of only the root's children, so a branch stays recognisable a few levels down.
