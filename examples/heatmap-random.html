Heatmap of random values

A 50 × 100 grid of random numbers, rendered to a canvas rather than to SVG so that grids of a few hundred cells on a side stay interactive. Cluster runs UPGMA clustering and animates the rows and columns into their new order; Resize redraws the same data at 1000 × 1000 pixels.
