Resizing a barplot

The same two samples as the taxonomy example, but built with width: 900, height: 300, barHeight: 60, maxItems: 8 and legend.columns: 4. Narrow and Wide call resize() with a different width, which lays the bars, the axis and the legend columns out again without rebuilding the barplot. Fit to stage passes the width the stage actually measures, which is what a page that follows its container does. The stage scrolls sideways whenever the barplot ends up wider than it is.
